# Salty Sisters — Design System

## Brand Essence
Women's community movement. Raw, real, warm. Inviting but bold. Spiritual depth without religious stiffness.

## Color Palette
- **Olive / Nature Green**: `#4A5D23` (deep), `#6B7F3B` (mid), `#8FA94E` (fresh), `#C5D68C` (light)
- **Sand / Beige**: `#F5F0E8` (background), `#EDE6D6` (warm), `#D9CEBC` (muted)
- **Orange Accent**: `#E07032` (primary CTA), `#F28C5A` (hover)
- **Dark**: `#1A1A18` (text), `#2D2D28` (secondary text)
- **White**: `#FEFDFB` (pure background)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: "Oswald" — condensed, bold, uppercase. Large poster-style.
- **Body**: "DM Sans" — clean, modern, friendly. Regular weight.
- **Accent**: Oswald light/italic for quotes or emphasis.

## Layout Principles
- Full-bleed sections with alternating backgrounds (white / sand / olive)
- Generous vertical padding (120px+ on desktop)
- Asymmetric compositions where appropriate
- Cards with subtle hover states, no heavy borders
- Horizontal scrolling for photo strips

## Motion
- Scroll-triggered reveals (staggered, bottom-up)
- Smooth parallax on hero video
- Subtle scale on card hovers
- Page load: logo fade → content reveal

## Photography Style (Placeholders)
- Warm, natural light
- Candid women in community
- Earth tones, outdoor settings
- Not overly polished — raw & authentic

## Map Style
- Muted/desaturated tiles (CartoDB Voyager or similar)
- Custom olive-colored markers
- Clean popup cards with group info

## i18n
- Default: German (de)
- Prepared: English (en)
- All user-facing strings in translation files
